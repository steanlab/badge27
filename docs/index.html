Tag LED and buttons: what they mean

Tag LED: green pulse = Bluetooth window open, waiting for the phone · blue steady = phone connected · no light = Bluetooth off · red flash = button, white flash = NFC tap. "Disconnect" leaves the window open for 30 s so you can reconnect; "Finish" switches the tag's Bluetooth off at once (open it again with a long press of the second button). While a phone is connected the buttons and NFC of the tag do nothing: use the buttons below.
